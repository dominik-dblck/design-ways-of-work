# Security

The browser is untrusted: the server decides who is asking and what they may do, at one door.

| Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Gate                                                                                                            | Exceptions                                       | Why                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Every route under `app/api` answers through `handle()` in `app/services/handleRouteService.ts`, which resolves the session first; there is no guard component and no client session (`useSession`, `SessionProvider`).                                                       | `everyHandlerCallsHandle` · `theEdgeIsTheOnlyDoor` · `nextAuthStaysAtTheEdge`                                   | the Auth.js route and the health check           | A guard in the UI protects pixels; the door protects the data.                                                                                 |
| The actor is the edge's: no route reads `by`, `actor` or `userId` off a request.                                                                                                                                                                                             | `theEdgeOwnsTheActor`                                                                                           | —                                                | Whoever the body says they are is whoever wrote the body.                                                                                      |
| The cookie carries the roster id and nothing that decides access; role, name and languages are read from the directory on every request. A claim is added to both minting sites, the `jwt` callback and `renewedSessionCookie`, or to neither.                               | `theCookieIsTheSession`                                                                                         | `picture`, the one fact the roster cannot answer | A change of role takes effect on the next request, not the next sign-in.                                                                       |
| No cookie, another secret, an expired one, a person the roster no longer holds: one 401 `unauthenticated`, the same answer for all four.                                                                                                                                     | `theCookieIsTheSession` · `theSessionLifetime`                                                                  | —                                                | Nothing on the wire says whether a session exists.                                                                                             |
| A session lives 8 hours rolling and 7 days at most; it is renewed past the half-life, on an answer that resolved a session, and a refusal or a 500 renews nothing.                                                                                                           | `theSessionLifetime`                                                                                            | —                                                | A refusal must not extend what it refused.                                                                                                     |
| The cookie's attributes (`HttpOnly`, `Secure` in production, `SameSite=Lax`) are declared once, in `readSessionCookieService`, and spelled once, in `serialiseCookieService`.                                                                                                | `oneCookieSerialiser` · `theCookieIsSerialisedOnce`                                                             | —                                                | Flags written twice drift apart.                                                                                                               |
| A write (`POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`) is same-origin or answered 403 `cross-site`: `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`, or an `Origin` equal to `AUTH_URL`'s; checked in `handle()` after the session and before the actor, under the stub too. `GET` is never checked.       | `theCookieIsTheSession` · `personaRoute.switchOff`                                                              | —                                                | The browser's own headers refuse a forgery without a client to mint a token; a browser client or a cross-origin partner brings the token back. |
| A forged write's 403 is `cross-site`, never the role's `forbidden`.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `theCookieIsTheSession`                                                                                         | —                                                | A caller told "your role may not" goes and fixes the wrong thing.                                                                              |
| The app refuses to start misconfigured: production without SSO, a missing `SSO_DOMAIN`, `AUTH_SECRET` or `AUTH_URL`, an empty `AUTH_GOOGLE_ID`, and in production a secret under 32 characters or an `AUTH_URL` that is not `https`, each throwing with the variable's name. | `resolveSessionService`                                                                                         | —                                                | The alternative is a 401 nobody can explain.                                                                                                   |
| Secrets stay on the server: `AUTH_*` is read in two files, no `'use client'` file imports `app/services/`, `DATABASE_ADMIN_URL` is named under neither `app/` nor `packages/`, and no token reaches a payload, a log or browser storage.                                     | `ssoCredentialsStayPut` · `noClientReadsTheEdge` · `theAdminLoginStaysOut` · `review` (payloads, logs, storage) | —                                                | Whatever reaches the browser, its owner can read.                                                                                              |
| Signing in writes nothing: no event, no command, no record.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `theLoginWritesNothing`                                                                                         | —                                                | Who may enter is the directory's answer, not the log's.                                                                                        |
