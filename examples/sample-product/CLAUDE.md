# Acme · a sample design product

The smallest design product the way of working's checks pass on. It names no real product: a new
design product starts from it, and the way of working runs its checks on it.

## Where things are

- **The homes** · taken from the way of working by `yarn wow`; never edited here.
- **Its own files** · `scope.ts`, `product.ts`, `productView.ts`, `sheet/project.ts`, and one file per
  registry the homes read.
- **Decided** `docs/process/decisions.ts` · **open** `roadmaps/findings.ts` · **its rules** `sheet/rules.ts`.
